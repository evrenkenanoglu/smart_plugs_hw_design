## Done when

A lab can identify the proposed product configuration, applicable test
questions, limitations of the simulations, and remaining evidence needed.

## Safety boundary

Simulation and documentation do not establish compliance or safety. All
energized mains EMC and safety testing must be performed by a properly
equipped, qualified lab. No improvised energized testing is part of this plan.
