## 1. 📋 System Specifications

*   **Application:** Heavy-duty AC load switching (Heaters, Motors, Appliances).
*   **Capacity:** 4 Channels x 16A (4000W max at 250V AC).
*   **Input Voltage:** 230V AC (50Hz/60Hz).
*   **Logic Power:** 5W Internal Isolated Supply (5V / 1A).
*   **Controller:** **ESP32-C6-WROOM-1-N8** (Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth LE, Surface Mount).
*   **PCB Specs:** 2-Layer FR-4, **1.6mm Thickness**, **2oz (70µm) Copper**.

## 2. ⚡ Electrical Design (Schematic)

### A. AC Logic Power Input (The "Safety Chain")
*Designed for EMC compliance and surge protection.*
1.  **Input:** Terminal Block `KF7.62-3P` (Live, Neutral, Earth Shield).
2.  **Protection:**
    *   **Fuse Holder (`FH1`):** `PTF-77` (Protects logic trace and PSU).
    *   **NTC (`NTC2`):** `10D-7` (10Ω). Limits inrush current.
    *   **MOV (`RV1`):** `14D431K` (430V). Clamps lightning/grid surges.
3.  **EMI Filtering:**
    *   **X2 Capacitor (`C1`, `C2`):** 100nF (Across L-N).
    *   **Common Mode Choke (`L1`):** `UU9.8-10mH` (Series).
    *   **Y-Capacitor (`CY1`):** 2.2nF **Y1 Class**. (Bridges AC Neutral $\to$ DC Ground).
4.  **Power Module:** Hi-Link **HLK-5LS05** (5W, 5V Output).

### B. DC Power Distribution
1.  **5V Rail:** Bulk filtered by 220µF Electrolytic (`C_BULK1/2`). Protected by a **5V TVS Diode** (`SMBJ5.0A`) against transient spikes.
2.  **3.3V Rail:** Generated by **AMS1117-3.3** LDO. Filtered by 10µF/22µF ceramics and an LC filter network featuring a 10µH Inductor (`L2`) for ESP32 RF stability.

### C. Relay Control & Output
*Repeated 4 times for channels 1-4.*
*   **Driver:** `SS8050` NPN Transistor (SOT-23). Driven via 1kΩ base resistor.
*   **Flyback Diode:** `B5819W SL` (Schottky SOD-123) for fast coil discharge.
*   **Relay (`K1-K4`):** Hongfa **HF115F-I/005-1HS3A** (16A high-inrush capability).
*   **Output Terminals:** PCB-mounted Faston Tabs `EST026` (`FT_IN1-4` & `FT_OUT1-4`).

### D. Digital Logic & Interface
*   **Microcontroller:** SMD **ESP32-C6-WROOM-1-N8** directly soldered to the PCB.
*   **Physical Controls:** 4x SMD Tactile Switches (`TS-1103ST-C-B`) for manual relay override.
*   **Programming Header (`P2`):** 6-Pin 2.54mm female header for UART flashing/debugging.
*   **Temperature Monitoring:** Onboard SMD Thermistor `HNTC0805-103F3950FB` to monitor enclosure/PCB temperature and trigger over-temperature shutdown.
*   **Expansion Ports:** 2x `XH-12A` (12-pin) headers for external status LEDs or auxiliary panels.

## 3. 📐 PCB Layout & Design Rules

**Material:** FR-4, **1.6mm**, **2oz Copper**, Blue Solder Mask.

### A. Trace Widths (Calculated for 2oz Copper)
| Net Function | Load | Rule Width | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AC Load** | 16A | **Polygon Pour** | ~10-12mm solid pour. **Direct Connect** (No thermal spokes). |
| **AC Mains Input** | 0.1A | **1.00 mm** | Mechanical robustness rule for the PSU feed. |
| **DC +5V Rail** | ~0.8A | **0.80 mm** | Copper pour / thick traces for relay coil bursts. |
| **DC +3.3V Rail** | ~0.5A | **0.40 mm** | LDO Output to ESP32-C6. |
| **Signals** | 0.05A | **0.25 mm** | ESP32 GPIO standard traces. |

### B. Safety & Isolation
*   **Air Gap:** Minimum **3.0mm** clearance + routing slots between AC High Voltage and DC Low Voltage regions.
*   **Polygon Styling:** AC High Current pads must use **Direct Connect** rules to prevent thermal relief spokes from melting under 16A loads.

## 5. 🛠️ Assembly & Wiring Guide

### PCB Assembly
1.  **SMD Soldering First:** The ESP32-C6-WROOM module, AMS1117, transistors, and 0805 passives must be flow-soldered or hand-soldered first. Ensure the ESP32 ground pad gets sufficient heat.
2.  **Faston Tabs (`EST026`):** Use a high-power soldering iron (75W+). The 2oz copper and Direct Connect polygons will absorb heat rapidly. Ensure full solder wetting for the 16A connections.
3.  **Programming:** The ESP32-C6 is surface mounted. Use the **P2 (1x6P Header)** with a standard USB-to-TTL UART adapter (3.3V logic) to flash the initial firmware.

### External Wiring (Safety Critical)
*   **Earth (PE):** Connect Wall Earth $\to$ Wago/Busbar $\to$ Output Sockets. **Do not run 16A Earth currents through the PCB.** Connect a small bridging wire to the PCB's `KF7.62` PE terminal strictly for logic-side EMI filtering/shielding.
*   **Neutral (N):** Wall Neutral $\to$ Sockets. Branch a low-current wire to the PCB's `KF7.62` input.
*   **Live (L):** Wall Live routes directly to the PCB `FT_IN1` through `FT_IN4` Faston tabs.
*   **Switched Live:** Connect wires from `FT_OUT1` through `FT_OUT4` to the respective output sockets. Make sure hookup wire is rated for 16A (minimum 1.5mm² / 14 AWG, preferably 2.5mm² / 12 AWG).
